# The 12-Week Program — Zero to Coverage-Ready

A structured path from "I don't have the financial literacy these postings ask for" to walking into a
Research Associate / infra-fund analyst interview with a portfolio of work. Pace assumes ~1 hour on weekdays
plus one longer weekend block (2–3 hrs) for Excel labs. Compress or stretch as needed — the sequence matters
more than the calendar.

**The daily loop (every weekday, ~45–60 min):**
1. 🎯 Daily Mission (12 mixed questions — keeps old material warm)
2. 🃏 All due flashcards (grade honestly; the scheduler does the rest)
3. One lesson + its drill from the current week's module (drill until ≥80%)
4. 🩹 Fix Your Misses whenever the bin has 10+

**The weekend block:** the week's case lab, done properly in Excel (not in your head), plus the listed
deliverable. Keep every Excel file — they become your interview portfolio.

## Phase 1 — The Language of Money (Weeks 1–2)

### Week 1 — Accounting mechanics
- Lessons/drills: all of **🧾 Accounting & the Three Statements**; clear the boss (≥75%).
- Flashcards: Accounting deck.
- **Lab:** none yet — drill the mechanics until the depreciation walk is reflexive.
- Deliverable: from memory, write the three-statement linking map on one page (IS→CFS→BS arrows).
  If you can't, re-read Lesson 1.

### Week 2 — Your first real model
- **Lab 1 (Three-Statement Build — GridWave)** in Excel, structured properly: assumptions block at top,
  IS / CFS / BS sections, every year balancing. Verify at the in-game checkpoints.
- Extensions (in `excel-labs.md`): add a revolver with minimum cash, interest income, a dividend switch.
- Deliverable: `gridwave_model.xlsx` with a balance check row reading TRUE across all years.
- Milestone check: Accounting boss ≥75%, Lab 1 complete, can answer "walk me through $10 of depreciation" out loud in <60 seconds.

## Phase 2 — Valuation (Weeks 3–5)

### Week 3 — DCF machinery
- Lessons/drills: **💸 Valuation & DCF** lessons 1–3; drills to ≥80%.
- Flashcards: Valuation deck.
- **Lab 2 (DCF — Meridian)** in Excel with a WACC block and a 5×5 WACC×g sensitivity table (Data Table).
- Deliverable: `meridian_dcf.xlsx` + three sentences: is the market's $28.50 defensible, and what would you need to believe?

### Week 4 — Method judgment
- Lesson 4 ("Which method for which asset") + Method drill; valuation boss ≥75%.
- Add mid-year convention and an exit-multiple TV toggle to your DCF; reconcile the two TVs.
- Writing rep #1: the "elevator pitch" writing drill in Module 7 (yes, early — writing starts now).

### Week 5 — Comps
- All of **📋 Comps & Comp Sheets**; boss ≥75%.
- **Lab 3 (Comp Sheet Friday)** in Excel from the raw table — TSM shares, EVs, multiples, growth, leverage —
  then write the five "READ it" bullets in your own words before opening the debrief.
- Deliverable: `power_comps.xlsx` formatted like you'd hand it to an MD (units labeled, one decimal on multiples, footnotes for MI).

## Phase 4 — Compute (Weeks 9–10)

### Week 9 — Data centers
- **🖥️ Data Centers** lessons 1–2, 5 + Data Center Math drill.
- **Lab 4 (Fort Nelson development)** in Excel with a cap-rate sensitivity row.
- Flashcards: Data Centers deck.
- Deliverable: a one-slide development underwrite card (cost, NOI, YoC, cap, profit, and the three IC questions).

### Week 10 — Neoclouds

- **Lab 5 (TensorForge)** in Excel; add a scenario toggle (contract rates −20/−40/−60% at renewal).

## Phase 5 — The Craft (Weeks 11–12)

### Week 11 — Thesis & writing
- All of **🎤 Thesis, Writing & the Process**; do all three writing drills seriously (type full drafts before
  revealing model answers); boss ≥75%.
- Start the **capstone** (below).

### Week 12 — Superday + capstone
- 🏁 Pass the **Superday** (≥75%). Re-run bosses that have decayed.
- Finish and rehearse the capstone.

## The Capstone (your interview portfolio)

Pick **one real public company** in the space (an IPP, a utility with data-center exposure, a data center REIT,
or a listed neocloud) and produce, from filings and IR materials only:

1. **A 3-statement model** (3 historical + 3 forecast years) — your Lab 1 skeleton, real numbers.
2. **A DCF + comp sheet** with 5–6 real peers — Labs 2–3 skeletons.
3. **A 2-page initiation-style note**: thesis (claim / variant view / catalysts / risks), valuation, the three
   numbers the debate turns on.
4. **A 90-second pitch** you can deliver cold, plus prepared answers to the five hardest questions against you
   (pre-mortem them).

This converts the training into proof. In interviews, "I built a model" is a claim; a workbook you can defend
line-by-line is evidence. When asked "how do you know the sector?", your answer is the capstone + the fact
pack habits from Module 7.

## Maintenance mode (after Week 12)

- 10 min/day: Daily Mission + due cards (the SRS intervals stretch to weeks — cheap to maintain).
- Weekly: update your capstone comp sheet after Friday's close; note what moved and why (this IS the associate job).
- Each earnings season: pick one covered-style company, do the 96-hour cycle from Module 7 Lesson 2 for real —
  preview note, variance table on print day, First Take within 2 hours.
- Follow the field guide in `resources.md` — 20 minutes of EIA/ISO/IR data per week keeps industry knowledge compounding.

Joel Kader

Thoughts on energy, infrastructure, and a world in transition.

Guided by curiosity, optimism, and respect for reality. Gladiatorial combat · LIB. II · CAPP. XX

This is a space for thoughts in motion; essays, notes, and fragments on energy, infrastructure, technology, capital, and the deeper systems shaping our world.

Every revolution begins with small fractures. Changing incentives, disruptive tools, and the gradual but constant reordering of the status quo. Slowly, quietly, then all at once.

It feels increasingly clear that we are entering a new era of building, one shaped not only by foundational technologies, but by the implications they carry for existing systems, institutions, and the physical world. Periods like these are rare. They ask for attention, reflection, and a willingness to engage with change.

This space is my effort to do so.
